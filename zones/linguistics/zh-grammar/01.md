Has no tone mark.

## Tone Changes

<ul>  
<li><b>3 + 1, 2, 4</b></li>  
voice is dropped as in 4 then continues on lower note<br>  
<span class='clHanzi'>買東西</span> - mǎi dōng xī - to buy things  
<br>  
<br>  
<li><b>3 + 3 (1 or 2 syllables)</b></li>  
3 tone is only the last one in the sequence, others are changed to 2<br>  
<span class='clHanzi'>買水果</span> - mǎi shuǐ guǒ - mái shuí guǒ - buy fruits  
<br>  
<br>  
<li><b>3 + 3 (more than 2 syllables)</b></li>  
odd tones are changed to 2, even tones remain 3, <br>  
<span class='clHanzi'>我有两把椅子</span> - wŏ yŏu liăng bă yĭzi - wó yŏu / liáng bá yĭzi - I have two chairs  
<br>  
<br>  
<li><span class='clHanzi'>一</span> yī + 1, 2, 3 = <b>4</b> tone</li>  
<li><span class='clHanzi'>一</span> yī + 4 =  <b>2</b> tone</b></li>  
<br>  
<li><span class='clHanzi'>不</span> bù + 1, 2, 3 = <b>4</b> tone</li>  
<li><span class='clHanzi'>不</span> bù + 4 =  <b>2</b> tone</b></li>
