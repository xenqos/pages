Grammar

Sandhi: 3rd Tone

3rd + 3rd

The 1st hanzi is pronounced in the 2nd tone.
The 2nd hanzi is pronounced in the 3rd tone.

你好   nǐ hǎo   hello
你好   ní hǎo

很好   hěn hǎo   very good
很好   hén hǎo

可以   kě yǐ  can / may
可以   ké yǐ

3rd + 3rd + 3rd

The 1st hanzi is pronounced in the 2nd tone.
The 2nd hanzi is pronounced in the 2nd tone.
The 3rd hanzi is pronounced in the 3rd tone.

我很好   wǒ hěn hǎo   I'm very well
我很好   wó hén hǎo

你很好   nǐ hěn hǎo   you are very good
你很好   ní hén hǎo

可以走   kě yǐ zǒu   can go / may leave
可以走   ké yí zǒu

3rd + 3rd + 3rd + 3rd

The 1st hanzi is pronounced in the 2nd tone.
The 2nd hanzi is pronounced in the 3rd tone.
The 3rd hanzi is pronounced in the 2nd tone.
The 4th hanzi is pronounced in the 3rd tone.

你也很好   nǐ yě hěn hǎo   you are also very good
你也很好   ní yě hén hǎo

我也想走   wǒ yě xiǎng zǒu   I also want to leave
我也想走   wó yě xiáng zǒu

可以找你   kě yǐ zhǎo nǐ   can look for you
可以找你   ké yǐ zháo nǐ
