Grammar

Sandhi: 一 yī

2nd + 4th

一 yī becomes 2nd tone before a 4th tone

一个   yī gè   one
一个   yí gè

一半   yī bàn   half
一半   yí bàn

一定   yī dìng   definitely
一定   yí dìng

4th + 1st, 2nd, 3rd

一 yī becomes 4th tone before a 1st, 2nd, 3rd tone

一天   yī tiān   a day
一天   yì tiān

一直   yī zhí   straight
一直   yì zhí

一起   yī qǐ   together
一起   yì qǐ
