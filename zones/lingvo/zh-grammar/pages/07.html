Syllable Structure

Syllable consists of:

  • initial - a consonant at the beginning of the syllable
  • final - a monophthong / diphthong / triphthong at the end of the syllable
Initial is optional.
Final is mandatory.

Finals:

  • Simple
  • Compound

Simple Final:

  • Vowels - a, o, e, i, u, ü
  • Diphthongs - ai, ei, ао, ou
  • Triphthongs - iao, uai, …

Compound Final:

  • medial (Semivowel) + Simple Final (ia, ie, iu, ua, uo, ui, üa, üe, iao, uai, …)
If initial is absent and final is - i, then y (semivowel) is added before i.
 yi is pronounced - [i].



If initial is absent and final is - u, then w (semivowel) is added before u.
 wu is pronounced - [u].
