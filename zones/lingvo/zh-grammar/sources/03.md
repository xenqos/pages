###### Grammar

# Syllables

## Syllable consists of:

[-
initial - a consonant at the beginning of the syllable
final - a monophthong / diphthong / triphthong at the end of the syllable
-]

Initial is optional.
Final is mandatory.

### Finals:

[-
Simple
Compound
-]

#### Simple Final:

[-
Vowels - %[r|a, o, e, i, u, ü]
Diphthongs - %[r|ai, ei, ао, ou]
Triphthongs -  %[r|iao, uai, …]
-]

#### Compound Final:

[-
medial (Semivowel) + Simple Final (%[r|ia, ie, iu, ua, uo, ui, üa, üe, iao, uai, …])
-]
