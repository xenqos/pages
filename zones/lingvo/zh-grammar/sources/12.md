###### Grammar

# Sandhi: 一 yī

### 2nd + 4th
一 yī becomes 2nd tone before a 4th tone

%[t1|一]%[t4|个] † yī gè † one
%[t2|一]%[t4|个] † yí gè

%[t1|一]%[t4|半] † yī bàn † half
%[t2|一]%[t4|半] † yí bàn

%[t1|一]%[t4|定] † yī dìng † definitely
%[t2|一]%[t4|定] † yí dìng

### 4th + 1st, 2nd, 3rd
一 yī becomes 4th tone before a 1st, 2nd, 3rd tone

%[t1|一]%[t1|天] † yī tiān † a day
%[t4|一]%[t1|天] † yì tiān

%[t1|一]%[t2|直] † yī zhí † straight
%[t4|一]%[t2|直] † yì zhí

%[t1|一]%[t3|起] † yī qǐ † together
%[t4|一]%[t3|起] † yì qǐ
