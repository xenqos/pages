###### Grammar

# Sandhi: 一 yī

### 2nd + 4th
一 yī becomes 2nd tone before a 4th tone

£1[一]£4[个] † yī gè † one
£2[一]£4[个] † yí gè

£1[一]£4[半] † yī bàn † half
£2[一]£4[半] † yí bàn

£1[一]£4[定] † yī dìng † definitely
£2[一]£4[定] † yí dìng

### 4th + 1st, 2nd, 3rd
一 yī becomes 4th tone before a 1st, 2nd, 3rd tone

£1[一]£1[天] † yī tiān † a day
£4[一]£1[天] † yì tiān

£1[一]£2[直] † yī zhí † straight
£4[一]£2[直] † yì zhí

£1[一]£3[起] † yī qǐ † together
£4[一]£3[起] † yì qǐ
