###### Grammar

# Sandhi: 3rd Tone

## 3rd + 3rd
The 1st hanzi is pronounced in the 2nd tone.
The 2nd hanzi is pronounced in the 3rd tone.

£3[你]£3[好] † nǐ hǎo † hello
£2[你]£3[好] † ní hǎo

£3[很]£3[好] † hěn hǎo † very good
£2[很]£3[好] † hén hǎo

£3[可]£3[以] † kě yǐ †can / may
£2[可]£3[以] † ké yǐ

## 3rd + 3rd + 3rd
The 1st hanzi is pronounced in the 2nd tone.
The 2nd hanzi is pronounced in the 2nd tone.
The 3rd hanzi is pronounced in the 3rd tone.

£3[我]£3[很]£3[好] † wǒ hěn hǎo † I'm very well
£2[我]£2[很]£3[好] † wó hén hǎo

£3[你]£3[很]£3[好] † nǐ hěn hǎo † you are very good
£2[你]£2[很]£3[好] † ní hén hǎo

£3[可]£3[以]£3[走] † kě yǐ zǒu † can go / may leave
£2[可]£2[以]£3[走] † ké yí zǒu

## 3rd + 3rd + 3rd + 3rd
The 1st hanzi is pronounced in the 2nd tone.
The 2nd hanzi is pronounced in the 3rd tone.
The 3rd hanzi is pronounced in the 2nd tone.
The 4th hanzi is pronounced in the 3rd tone.

£3[你]£3[也]£3[很]£3[好] † nǐ yě hěn hǎo † you are also very good
£2[你]£3[也]£2[很]£3[好] † ní yě hén hǎo

£3[我]£3[也]£3[想]£3[走] † wǒ yě xiǎng zǒu † I also want to leave
£2[我]£3[也]£2[想]£3[走] † wó yě xiáng zǒu

£3[可]£3[以]£3[找]£3[你] † kě yǐ zhǎo nǐ † can look for you
£2[可]£3[以]£2[找]£3[你] † ké yǐ zháo nǐ
