###### Grammar

# Syllable Structure

## Syllables ##

## Syllable consists of:

[-
initial - a consonant at the beginning of the syllable
final - a monophthong / diphthong / triphthong at the end of the syllable
-]

Initial is optional.
Final is mandatory.

### Finals:

[-
Simple
Compound
-]

#### Simple Final:

[-
Vowels - %[r|a, o, e, i, u, ü]
Diphthongs - %[r|ai, ei, ао, ou]
Triphthongs -  %[r|iao, uai, …]
-]

#### Compound Final:

[-
medial (Semivowel) + Simple Final (%[r|ia, ie, iu, ua, uo, ui, üa, üe, iao, uai, …])
-]

If initial is absent and final is - %[r|i], then %[r|y] (semivowel) is added before %[r|i].<br>&emsp;%[r|yi] is pronounced - [i].</p>

If initial is absent and final is - %[r|u], then %[r|w] (semivowel) is added before %[r|u].<br>&emsp;%[r|wu] is pronounced - [u].</p>
