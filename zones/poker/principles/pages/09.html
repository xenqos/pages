Player Styles

Loose

  Play many hands
  Play weak hands

Tight

  Play strong hands
  Fold weak hands

Passive

  Call frequently
  Raise rarely

Aggressive

  Bet frequently
  Raise frequently
  Call rarely

LAG

  Loose Aggressive

TAG

  Tight Aggressive

Guidelines

if
  Table - Loose / Aggressive
then
  Play Tighter
endif

if
  Table - Tight / Passive
then
  Play Looser
endif
