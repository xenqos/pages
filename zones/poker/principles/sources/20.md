# Basic Strategy

## Rule 1
Play hands strictly according to hand strength (WinRate).

## Rule 2
Continue on the Flop only if it improved your hand or you are the Aggressor.

## Rule 3
Play a drawing hand only if you have 4 cards to a straight/flush after the Flop.

## Rule 4
Play aggressively with Top Pair / Big Kicker (TPBK) or an Overpair post-Flop.

## Rule 5
Reassess the board texture and opponents' ranges after every street.

## Rule 6
Raise or fold. Never limp.

## Rule 7
Fold when beaten. Minimize losses, do not chase.

## Rule 8
Respect table position; play tighter early, looser late.
